Monday · Week 01

What Does It Mean to Be Healthy?

Beyond the absence of disease

Health & human experience
MBS-1-2026

Can someone live with a chronic disease and still experience meaningful health?

Health includes more than test results: daily functioning, adaptation, quality of life, and social circumstances matter alongside clinical indicators.

سلامت فقط نبود بیماری نیست؛ شاخص‌های بالینی در کنار توانایی انجام فعالیت‌های روزمره، کیفیت زندگی، سازگاری و شرایط اجتماعی معنا پیدا می‌کنند.

Read on LinkedIn ↗

Key idea

The WHO’s classical definition describes health as physical, mental, and social well-being. Huber and colleagues proposed understanding health also as the ability to adapt and self-manage when facing challenges. This perspective complements rather than replaces diagnosis and clinical assessment.

Discussion prompt

How should healthcare outcomes measure patients’ everyday functioning alongside clinical indicators?

Huber, M., et al. (2011). “How should we define health?” BMJ, 343, d4163. DOI: 10.1136/bmj.d4163

Tuesday · Week 01

From Cells to Disease

How molecular changes disrupt human health

Cellular senescence
MBS-2-2026

When can a cell that stops dividing become a problem?

Cellular senescence can protect against uncontrolled cell growth and support repair, but persistent senescent cells may release signals that impair tissue function.

پیری سلولی می‌تواند نقش محافظتی داشته باشد؛ اما تجمع سلول‌های پیر و ترشح عوامل التهابی از مسیر SASP ممکن است در اختلال عملکرد بافت‌ها نقش داشته باشد. درمان‌های هدف‌گیرنده هنوز در حال بررسی‌اند.

Read on LinkedIn ↗

Key idea

Senescent cells remain metabolically active while stably exiting the cell cycle. Their effects depend on context: senescence can help suppress tumors and support tissue repair, while persistent cells and their senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP) may contribute to inflammation and age-related dysfunction.

Research in progress

Senolytics are being investigated, but clinical benefits and long-term safety in humans remain under study.

Gorgoulis, V., et al. (2019). “Cellular Senescence: Defining a Path Forward.” Cell, 179(4), 813–827. DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2019.10.005

Wednesday · Week 01

The Human Microbiome

A hidden partner in health and disease

Microbiology & human health
MBS-3-2026

If the microbiome changes during illness, did it cause the illness?

Microbial communities interact with immunity and metabolism, but an observed difference between patients and healthy people does not establish cause and effect.

میکروبیوم با ایمنی و سوخت‌وساز بدن تعامل دارد و تحت تأثیر بیماری، رژیم غذایی و دارو تغییر می‌کند؛ اما مشاهده تفاوت میکروبی به‌تنهایی رابطه علت و معلولی را ثابت نمی‌کند.

Read on LinkedIn ↗

Key idea

The Integrative Human Microbiome Project tracked microbial and human biological changes over time in conditions including inflammatory bowel disease, pregnancy, and prediabetes. Microbiome composition is dynamic, and findings need careful interpretation: disease, treatment, and lifestyle can also reshape microbial communities.

Discussion prompt

How can researchers distinguish microbes that drive disease from those that respond to it?

Integrative HMP Research Network Consortium (2019). “The Integrative Human Microbiome Project.” Nature, 569, 641–648. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-019-1238-8

Thursday · Week 01

Inflammation: Defense or Threat?

When the body’s defense becomes harmful

Immunology
MBS-4-2026

Why can a response that protects us also cause harm?

Acute inflammation helps fight infection and repair tissue. Persistent inflammatory signaling can disrupt tissue function, so restoring balance matters as much as activating defense.

التهاب حاد برای دفاع و ترمیم ضروری است؛ اما تداوم آن ممکن است به عملکرد بافت‌ها آسیب بزند. مهار گسترده التهاب نیز می‌تواند دفاع ایمنی و ترمیم را مختل کند.

Read on LinkedIn ↗

Key idea

Inflammatory pathways interact with metabolic and vascular processes and are associated with several chronic diseases. These relationships vary by tissue and condition; inflammation is not the sole cause of every disease. Research examines how responses are switched off and actively resolved, not only how to suppress them.

Discussion prompt

Could promoting inflammation resolution offer benefits over broadly suppressing immune responses?

Furman, D., et al. (2019). “Chronic inflammation in the etiology of disease across the life span.” Nature Medicine, 25, 1822–1832. DOI: 10.1038/s41591-019-0675-0

Friday · Week 01

Lifestyle, Environment and Disease

Can prevention change the future of medicine?

Preventive medicine
MBS-5-2026

Is prevention only a matter of individual choice?

Diet, physical activity, pollution exposure, and healthcare access shape health risks. Effective prevention combines personal support with healthier environments and public-health measures.

پیشگیری فقط به انتخاب فردی وابسته نیست؛ قیمت غذا، آلودگی، طراحی محیط شهری و دسترسی به خدمات سلامت نیز اثرگذارند. پیشگیری مؤثر به مراقبت بالینی و سیاست‌گذاری عمومی نیاز دارد.

Read on LinkedIn ↗

Key idea

A global analysis using Global Burden of Disease estimates associated dietary risks with substantial population-level mortality and disability. These estimates do not predict an individual’s outcome or prove that changing one food will prevent disease. Prevention also depends on the conditions that make healthier choices possible.

Discussion prompt

Should healthcare systems invest more in reducing environmental risks as well as treating disease?

GBD 2017 Diet Collaborators (2019). “Health effects of dietary risks in 195 countries, 1990–2017.” The Lancet, 393, 1958–1972. DOI: 10.1016/S0140-6736(19)30041-8

Saturday · Weekly synthesis

Understanding Human Health and Disease

Five perspectives, one connected system

Weekly report
MBS-WR1-2026

What connects cellular stress, gut microbes, inflammation, diet, and the meaning of health?

Health emerges from interactions across molecular, cellular, microbial, immune, behavioral, and social levels. No single diagnosis or biomarker captures the whole picture.

سلامت حاصل تعامل فرایندهای مولکولی، سلولی، میکروبی، ایمنی، رفتاری و اجتماعی است. پزشکی دقیق‌تر باید این سطوح را به هم پیوند دهد، بدون آن‌که تجربه و شرایط زندگی فرد را نادیده بگیرد.

Read weekly post ↗

The connecting idea

Clinical measures remain essential, but they gain meaning when considered alongside physiological function, environmental exposure, daily life, and social context. Cellular senescence, microbiome shifts, inflammation, and prevention are related perspectives—not interchangeable explanations for every disease.

Discussion prompt

How can healthcare integrate molecular evidence, prevention, and patients’ lived experience without oversimplifying disease?

López-Otín, C., et al. (2023). “Hallmarks of Health.” Cell, 186(1), 33–63. DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2022.11.001

Weekly synthesis

Health is a connected story.

This week’s posts move from the meaning of health to the cellular, microbial, immune, and environmental processes that influence it. The shared lesson is not that one factor explains disease, but that health emerges from interacting systems.

Good science communication keeps both the promise and the limitations of research visible: association is not causation, preclinical promise is not an approved treatment, and population estimates do not determine an individual outcome.
